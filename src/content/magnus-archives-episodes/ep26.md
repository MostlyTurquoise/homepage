# A Distortion

This episode continues the "Worms" storyline, notably with the introduction of conflicting themes explicitly occuring within the episode - the distorted "Michael" (seemingly a continuation of the "Body Horror" theme as seen in "The Bone-Turner's Tale") helps S.G. (the archive assistant Sasha James) successfully fend off and kill an instance of worms infesting a person, by using a CO2 fire extinguisher. 

## Summary

A seemingly human who introduces himself as "Michael", but is in reality a strangely distorted humanoid figure with stretched hands and sharp fingers where bones poke through (only visible as such when viewed in reflections or through warped glass), approaches S.G. (archive assistant Sasha James) offering to help her against the "Flesh Hive", seemingly what has infested Jane Prentiss. He invites her to meet him at a graveyard, then shows her from there to a room with an infested man. She discovers that using a CO2 fire extinguisher kills the worms. "Michael" cuts into her arm following the encounter to remove a worm which had burrowed in without her noticing.
