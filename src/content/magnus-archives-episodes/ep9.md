# A Father's Love

[Initial notes have been lost]

## Summary

S.G.'s mother goes missing at night, leaving behind necklace with closed eye and hand symbol. S.G. wakes her father. When taps are run, brown seawater flows rather than normal water, and the light in the kitchen blows out. S.G.'s father leaves and returns at a later point. No missing person's report is logged. S.G.'s father becomes a serial killer who kills people at the will of some unknown individual. All his victims have symbols drawn on their bodies. Events culminate when S.G. is woken at night. She goes downstairs but finds that the taps are flowing with seawater. Lights go out down the street getting closer and closer, and the darkness seems too dark. Once all lights are out there is knocking on the door, which gets progressively harder and culminates in the door breaking. S.G. calls the police before running out to the garden. S.G. finds her father in the middle of some ritual with a victim. The victim's heart has been removed and is held by S.G.'s father, but it is still beating, as are all the hearts in jars on shelves in the room. They all glow with blue light. The body is wearing the same kind of necklace that the S.G.'s mother had. S.G.'s father stabs the heart and the darkness ends, as does the glowing blue light. He is arrested following a manhunt.
