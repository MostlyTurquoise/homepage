# A Sturdy Lock

This episode seems mostly self-contained.

## Summary

S.G., an elderly man, is kept awake at night by an intruder attempting to break into his room. When police are called, there is no sign of forced entry, and when people stay over, the effects do not manifest. The intruder is only kept away by a lock on the bedroom door, but the post-statement analysis reveals there was no lock on the bedroom door. The intrusions are also not visible on cameras placed within the house.
