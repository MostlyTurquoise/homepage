# Strange Music

This episode seems quite self-contained, although potentially provides a part of the "Music" theme due to similarities to the previous statement "The Piper", where those who are doomed by the occurences of the statement hear music that is an omen of their imminent death. The differences being that the Piper seems to just herald death by war, whereas the Calliope music of this statement seems to herald death by having your jaw removed by a weird clown doll, which are somewhat thematically different. Additionally, the removal workers at the end could be Breekon & Hope, which would line up considering they are recurringly involved in the movement of objects related to these occurences.

## Summary

S.G. uncovers an old Calliope steam organ and box of dolls with jaws missing in the attic of her grandfather's house following his passing. The box seems to reopen itself and a clown doll seems to look at her. She plays an old tune on the organ, which works despite the lack of steam supply. When she plays this for her at the time boyfriend, he becomes distressed. He reportedly hears the music following the event, in the distance but slowly getting closer. A doll appears which looks like him. He is found dead with his jaw ripped off, and his doll is similarly damaged. The steam organ and trunk are stolen by two men who look like official removal workers. The Magnus Institute is now in possession of the steam organ, through unknown means, although it is now locked(?).
