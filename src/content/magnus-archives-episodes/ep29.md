# Cheating Death

I am not currently sure where to file this episode. The repeated mentions of death might indicate a filing under "Graves", although there is very little mention of graves themselves. The mention of sharp bony hands being used to kill seems to line up with bone-related areas of the "Body Horror" theme, although this is also tenuous. As such, I am leaving this uncategorised for the time being.

## Summary

S.G. was a soldier during the American Civil War(?) and ended up wounded and lost in fog and rain as it gets dark. He finds an abandoned farmhouse, and in the cellar finds a skeleton dressed in monk's robes sat at a table. The skeleton is in some way in incarnation of death(?). He plays Pharoah against it and cheats to win, believing he will not die as a result. Instead, he takes the place of the monk, becoming a skeleton, and the monk becomes flesh and blood and returns into the world. S.G. takes his place and kills various people and plays games with them, until he is eventually beaten and so becomes human and immortal. He now cannot die, but cannot eat, drink, or sleep either. S.G. could not be traced for a follow up.
