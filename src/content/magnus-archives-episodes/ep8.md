# Burned Out

[Initial notes have been lost]

## Summary

S.G. is working on a house being renovated / rebuilt. The house seems to be haunted by the original owner who died by burning(?). The S.G. experiences extreme heat, and the ghost of the owner seems to burn away into just a scorch mark. A priest visits and attempts an exorcism (See "Confession"). Mention of a person named Agnes who is seemingly tied to the fire, and dies when the events of the episode conclude. The electrician pulls down a tree which bleeds and underneath is buried a box with an apple in. Once uncovered, the apple rots and turns into spiders.
