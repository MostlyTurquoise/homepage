# Schwartzwald

This episode seems to create some important links between things, notably introducing the founder of the Magnus Institute, Jonah Magnus. It also presents some sort of genealogy for Mary and Gerard Keay, being descendants of the S.G.'s nephew. I have filed it under "Eyes", as they show up throughout the episode.

## Summary

S.G. finds a mausoleum of "Johann von Wuerttemburg" in an old abandoned graveyard in the woods. His tomb is surrounded with bookshelves with small open eyes engraved into them, although all books have decayed. S.G. encounters a strange man with a wide brim hat who is later revealed to have no eyes, who warns him about progressing further. There are a book and coin on the floor which have survived. The book is in arabic, and the coin has "JW" and "1279" inscribed. S.G.'s nephew is an ancestor of Mary and Gerard Keay.
