# Growing Darkness

This episode links the closed eye and hand symbol found in e.g. "A Father's Love" to a name - a cult that Johnathan Sims refers to as "The People's Church of the Divine Host". This emblem and the repeated appearance of darkness being linked means I am firmly filing this under the "Darkness" theme. It also provides the first instance of a Darkness "pocket dimension" (pending better term).

## Summary

S.G.'s girlfriend's roommate joins a cult (see above), and becomes averse to light. She un-tightens all the lightbulbs in the flat so the lights don't work, and exclusively is active at night. At one point, she starts singing and pacing at night, but does not elaborate when S.G.'s girlfriend interrupts her. Following culminating events, S.G. goes to try and confront the roommate but she is gone - he goes to a chapel named on a note in her room. When he enters, his torch goes out, and he finds himself in a much larger space of pure darkness with smooth marble floor. There is chanting / singing from all directions. He walks for a little bit before coming across a grate, but when he puts his hands against it fingers reach through and try to grab him. He remembers he has his phone, which when he turns on the phone torch, returns him to the original chapel.
