# Skintight

Aside from characters with skin that they removed and put back on (notably previously being Mary Keay), this episode seems quite independent.

## Summary

S.G. is a content creator and paranormal investigator. She hires a last minute sound technician for an episode, who permanently smokes and has the smell of some kind of perfume about her. The sound tech acts weird and when they arrive at the site they will be filming, she gets upset. During the night, when it is the sound tech's shift, S.G. awakes to find her missing. S.G.o gets a night vision camera and uses it to navigate the property. She comes across the room where the sound tech is, the sound tech seemingly speaking to someone in the dark who doesn't show up to the S.G.. The sound tech is apologising, and eventually gets picked up by whatever she is talking to and thrown against the back wall. She is seemingly injured. S.G. then observes her taking off the skin on her arm, reapplying it, and stapling it into place.
