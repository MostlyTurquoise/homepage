# Killing Floor

This episode has led me to think "meat" is maybe its own theme - it has showed up twice (see "The Man Upstairs") and that is enough for me. It also has some degree of "pocket dimension" in this episode, which I am toying with as criteria for a theme being official and not just a pattern.

## Summary

A worker at a slaughterhouse is working on the killing floor. When he stops getting new animals coming in, he notices that the slaughterhouse has changed - it is clean, and none of the doors lead to places he recognises. He wanders the corridoors which are mostly identical, before encountering a staircase, which he climbs. He finds a door that leads out onto the slaughtering conveyor, but quickly returns back from it. He follows the smell of blood to find a large room filled with dead carcasses of animals, all being churned down into some machine. He heads in another direction and hears the sound of the bolt gun, and returns to the killing floor to find his coworker using the boltgun on himself. The coworker hands S.G. the boltgun and guides him to kill him with it. S.G. kills his coworker, then finds he has escaped the strange alternate slaughterhouse. He leaves and resigns. It is revealed that in reality, both him and his coworker left the slaughterhouse during their shift and his coworker was not seen afterwards. Neither of them interacted with other employees on their ways out. The slaughterhouse is currently being expanded, but various builders have quit, citing potentially similar occurences to what happened to the S.G..
