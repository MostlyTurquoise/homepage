# Do Not Open

[Initial notes have been lost]

## Summary

S.G. agrees to look after mysterious coffin with scratching and moaning inside. It feels warm and is locked with a cool silver chain and lock. He subconsciously / in his dreams tries to open the coffin, so freezes the key in ice for the year he keeps the coffin. Eventually, the original owner picks it up with Breekon & Hope, and the original owner presumably is taken into the coffin.

## Further notes

30/10/2026 - This episode introduces both the "Graves" theme and the company Breekon & Hope, who show up later on in various places to do with the supernatural.
