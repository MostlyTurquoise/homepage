Over the past few weeks I have become quite interested in the horror anthology podcast The Magnus Archives. I am listening to it blind, and as I do I am making notes on my theories. I have made a page that lists each episode and lets you view my thoughts.

The only material I am looking at for my theorising is:

- The official podcast audio
- Podcast episode descriptions
- The wiki content warnings list
- The wiki list of characters by episode

Which should let me minimise the amount of spoilers I get, while still making the experience enjoyable.
